# About Me
{: #about-me }

Hi! I’m Zijie Jin, a Ph.D. candidate at the [School of Computer Science](https://scs.bupt.edu.cn/), Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT), supervised by [Shuai Zhao](https://teacher.bupt.edu.cn/zhaoshuai). Before that, I received my B.Eng. degree in Automation from the [School of Automation and Electrical Engineering](https://saee.ustb.edu.cn/) at the University of Science and Technology Beijing (USTB) in 2026, under the supervision of [Qing Li](https://saee.ustb.edu.cn/szdw/xsjs2/kzkxygcx/346c13ee30504a0cb64c4bc213693ee2.htm).

My research interests include video understanding, multimodal learning and embodied intelligence.

If you’d like to explore a research idea together or pool GPU resources for a shared experiment, I’d love to hear from you.
